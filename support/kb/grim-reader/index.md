<div lang="en" markdown="1">

Grim Reader is a reading app for your own book collection on iPhone and iPad. You
get books from a source you manage yourself, then read them offline. The app has
no store, no ads and no third-party account.

This guide follows version 5.70 of the app. Tap a chapter to open it.

## Contents

1. [Adding books](boeken-toevoegen.html) — from a file, a WebDAV server, an OPDS
   catalog or a Grimmory server
2. [Settings](instellingen.html) — every setting, by category
    - [Reading and listening](instellingen.html#lezen-en)
    - [Appearance and templates](instellingen.html#weergave-en)
    - [Files and metadata](instellingen.html#bestanden-en)
    - [Sources and accounts](instellingen.html#bronnen-en)
    - [AI](instellingen.html#ai-en)
    - [Reading overview](instellingen.html#leesoverzicht-en)
    - [Storage](instellingen.html#opslag-en)
    - [Language, privacy and more](instellingen.html#taal-en)
3. [Library management](bibliotheekbeheer.html) — improving data, cleaning up and
   merging, maintenance

Coming soon: reading (EPUB, PDF, comics), audiobooks and read-aloud, and
organizing your library with favorites and collections.

Stuck? Go to **[Help &amp; support](/support/)**.
